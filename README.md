## Dashboard Analysis-
## Key Insights:
### 1. Government Segment Drives the Highest Sales-
      - The Government segment contributes 44.22% of total sales, making it the largest revenue-generating customer group.
      - Small Business customers account for 35.74% representing the second-largest segment.
      -Enterprise and Midmarket segments contribute significantly less.
### 2. United States and Canada Lead Sales Performance-
      - The United States and Canada are the strongest- performing markets.
      - Mexico generates the lowest sales among the displayed countries.
      - European markets (France and Germany) remain highly competitive.
### 3. Paseo Dominates Product Sales-
      - Paseo (sales- $33M) significantly performs better than other products.
### 4. Discount Strategy Shows Balanced Distribution-
       - Most sales occur under discounted conditions.
       - Only a small portions of revenue is generated without discounts.
### 5. France Generates the highest Profit - 
      - France profit     $ 3.78M
      - Germany profit    $ 3.68M
      -Canada profit      $ 3.53M
      - USA profit         $ 3.00M
      - Mexico profit      $ 2.91M
## Recommendations:
###  1.Focus on Government customers that generate  the Most Revenue-
           Give low discounts on all government segments.
### 2. Replicate success in low- performing markets:
      - Analyze successful sales strategies used in the France and Germany.
      - Apply similar approaches in Mexico and USA.
### 3. Grow Sales of Lower-Selling Products :
       - Promote VVT, Velo and Amarilla through targeted campaigns.
       - Bundle lower-performing products with Paseo.
### 4.   Optimize Discount Policies:
       - Evaluate profitability by discount band.
       - Reduce unnecessary high-discount promotions.
